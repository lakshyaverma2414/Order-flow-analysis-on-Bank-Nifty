# Limitations

The conclusions in this repository should be read as strong evidence **within the sampled setting**, not as a universal statement about all options markets.

## Scope Limitations
- the dataset covers a single index-options market rather than a cross-asset universe
- the sample spans 27 trading days, which is substantial for a focused microstructure study but not a multi-year regime cycle
- the analysis is confined to the available top-of-book and trade fields, without richer order-book depth or participant tagging

## External Validity
- the study does not test whether the same null result holds across other underlyings, exchanges, or volatility regimes
- cross-market confirmation, especially across equity index, single-stock, and commodity options, would strengthen or qualify the conclusion

## Extension Paths
- cross-asset validation
- longer sample windows covering additional macro regimes
- richer state variables, including deeper book information and cross-instrument flow

These limits do not weaken the honesty of the result. They simply define where the current evidence ends and where future empirical work could begin.
